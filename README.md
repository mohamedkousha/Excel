# Excel


In this branch of my GitHub portfolio, I have chosen to collect, categorize, and explore in depth all the main functions of Microsoft Excel, creating a structured space designed not only to showcase my knowledge of the tool but also to highlight my methodical approach to organization and technical documentation. My goal was to build an archive that goes far beyond a simple list of formulas, transforming it instead into a thoughtful journey through the logic that drives Excel, its capabilities, and its real‑world applications in data analysis, reporting, and process automation.

Within this branch, I am progressively adding functions from every category—mathematical, statistical, logical, text‑based, date and time, lookup and reference, and even the most recent dynamic functions—each accompanied by detailed explanations, practical examples, and helpful notes to clarify how they work in real scenarios. The objective is to create a resource that reflects my ability to analyze complex tools, break them down into understandable components, and present them in a clear, organized, and professional way.

This space also serves as a way to demonstrate my attention to precision, clarity, and detail: every function is described with care, including its parameters, differences between language versions, common use cases, and situations where it can be combined with other formulas to achieve more advanced results. I want anyone visiting this branch to perceive not only my technical competence but also my ability to communicate complex concepts in an accessible and meaningful manner.
